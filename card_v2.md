**Title:** Bagerhat Heritage Conservation: DPP and Terms of Reference

**Summary:** Our 6-member team drafted a government-format Development Project Proposal (DPP) and a consultant Terms of Reference (ToR) for a heritage conservation master plan for the Mosque City of Bagerhat. The package includes a BDT 300 lakh budget, a log frame, staffing and a 36-month phased work plan.
